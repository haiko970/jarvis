# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript in one folder (`biga/`), no framework, no build step, fonts and images served from the folder itself. Chosen by the user.

## Users

People in and around Le Pouliguen (residents, holidaymakers on the Côte d'Amour) deciding where to eat or what to order tonight, mostly on a phone, often already hungry. They want to see the pizzas, the prices, the opening hours and the phone number, then call.

## Product Purpose

The website of Biga, a pizzeria in Le Pouliguen (sur place ou à emporter). It shows the whole menu in a way people remember and makes calling to order one tap away. Success: a visitor finds their pizza, its price and the phone number within seconds, and calls.

## Positioning

A small neighbourhood pizzeria with a handwritten, kraft-paper identity ("Best pizza in town" on the menu), wood-fired style pizzas with Italian products (fior di latte, grana padano AOP, burrata, spianata, taleggio, mortadelle).

## Operating Context

Orders are taken by phone and on site; there is no online ordering. Lunch offer: "Formule du midi" (sandwich + drink). Instagram is the brand's social channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real business: every fact (phone, address, hours, prices, ingredients) must match the menu exactly. Do not invent claims, reviews, awards, delivery or online ordering.
- Content language: French.
- Facts from the menu (source: photo sent by the user, 2026-11-17):
  - Téléphone 09 71 51 95 42 · 20 boulevard de l'Atlantique, Le Pouliguen (44510)
  - Mardi au samedi, 12h–13h30 et 18h30–21h30 · Sur place ou à emporter
  - Instagram @biga_pizza_44510
  - Formule du midi 11 € : une boisson et un sandwich, tous les midis de la semaine
  - Sandwichs (uniquement le midi) 8,50 € : Spinata, Jambon cru, Légumes marinés, Mortadelle
  - 20 pizzas from 10 € to 18 € (full list in `biga/index.html` data)
  - Suppléments : burrata 5 €, œuf ou légume 1 €, viande ou fromage 1,50 €
- Undecided: hosting / domain, legal notices (mentions légales), real photos of each pizza.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "BIGA", cartoon pizza-slice mascot logo, tagline "Best pizza in town" (from the menu).
- Menu identity: kraft cardboard paper, black ink hand-drawn illustrations, heavy condensed headings.

## Evidence on Hand

- `biga/img/pizza-parme.webp`: one real photo of a pizza (jambon cru, burrata, roquette on a green base), sent by the user.
- The printed menu (photo sent by the user, not shipped).
- No photo of the other pizzas, of the room or of the team; no logo file. Pizza visuals are drawn in code until real photos exist. No reviews or press to quote.

## Product Principles

1. The menu is the product: every pizza, its ingredients and its price, exactly as printed.
2. Calling is the action: the phone number is always one tap away.
3. Feels like Biga's own kraft-and-ink menu, not a template.
4. Works first on a phone in a hurry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

French UI text, keyboard usable, respects reduced motion, readable contrast on kraft.
