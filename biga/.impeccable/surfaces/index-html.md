# Surface: biga/index.html — the menu shelf

Mode: Experience (the menu itself is the artifact; the call to order is always one tap away).
Audience and job: hungry locals and holidaymakers in Le Pouliguen, mostly on phones; find a pizza, its price, call.
Constraints: every fact from the printed menu, verbatim prices and ingredients; no online ordering, no invented claims; French copy.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The user's VHS filmography-shelf brief, pinned whole and translated to pizza boxes: an endless shelf of Biga's kraft pizza boxes standing on edge, side wall out; the active pizza's box is pulled out and turned in 3D so its printed lid faces you. Refuses the category default of a photo hero plus a scrolling price list.
OWN-WORLD: Biga's kraft menu as a single-colour flexo-printed pizza box: kraft ground #D6BF98, darker box kraft, ink #17130F, cobalt stamp ink #1F3FB0 (the brief's cobalt kept as the box print colour), lid illustrations as misregistered screen prints in ink, vermilion sauce, cream and basil green. Boxed catalogue labels in Inter Tight, Newsreader for names and ingredient prose, Big Shoulders Display 900 for printed box lettering, Caveat Brush only for the menu's "Best pizza in town".
STORY: The visitor sees the whole menu as boxes, flips through pizzas, reads ingredients and price, opens one, then taps to call.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (call button left, cobalt BIGA logo centre, hours right); base tabs above the shelf; shelf across the middle with the active box turned out at -4.5°, lid illustrated; caption under it (name, key ingredients, price); arrows; footer with address, Instagram, "Le midi & infos".
FORM: brief-pinned VHS shelf → pizza-box shelf; signature interaction: box turns out of the shelf, lid opens to a full-screen sheet with ink-blot reveal. seed: none (pinned) — "Give me the superdetailed exact prompt to recreate this page exactly as it is" / "En t'appuyant sur le prompt que je viens de t'envoyer, tu vas me créer un site pour la pizzeria Biga au Pouliguen … À la place de livres, ce sera bien sûr des pizzas."
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Translation notes: tape → pizza box (square lid, spine = side wall, ws = 0.14 H, d = H); decades → bases (Base tomate / Base crème / Pesto & épinard); year → price; romaji/kanji → key ingredients; "OPEN / 開く" → "OUVRIR / LA BOÎTE"; "Watch the film" → "Appeler pour commander"; BACK → call button. Real photo used for the Parme detail; other pizzas drawn in code (to replace with real photos later).
